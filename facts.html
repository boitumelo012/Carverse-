🧠 Did You Know?

Here are some fun, surprising, and educational facts about cars from around the world.

Fact #1

The Bugatti Chiron Super Sport 300+ is the first production car to hit over 300 mph — that’s faster than a cheetah!

Fact #2

In Italy, it’s illegal to drive a car without a valid license plate.

Fact #3

The average modern car has over 30 computers controlling everything from fuel injection to airbags.

Fact #4

Hybrid cars were first built in the late 1800s — long before electric cars became popular again.

Fact #5

Some luxury cars have more software code than a Boeing 787 Dreamliner airplane.

Fact #6

The first car ever made was by Karl Benz in 1885 — it had just one cylinder and could go up to 10 mph.
